# Equity Research & Financial Valuation: Kohinoor Chemical Company (Bangladesh) PLC

## Executive Summary
This repository contains the equity research report and financial valuation model for **Kohinoor Chemical Company (Bangladesh) PLC (DSE: KOHINOOR)**, the 
pioneer FMCG manufacturer of personal care, cosmetics, and household hygiene products in Bangladesh (established in 1956). 

The research evaluates Kohinoor's financial health (FY2020–FY2025), industry competitiveness, macro drivers, 
and intrinsic share value using multiple valuation methodologies.

---

## Key Valuation Results

* **Current Market Price (CMP):** BDT 533.10
* **Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC):** 13.80%
* **Cost of Equity ($K_e$):** 14.05% | **Cost of Debt ($K_d$):** 2.45% (Pre-tax)

| Valuation Methodology | Model Intrinsic Value (BDT) | Valuation Verdict vs CMP (533.10) |
|---|---|---|
| **Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)** | **BDT 150.14** | Overvalued |
| **Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE)** | **BDT 133.03** | Overvalued |
| **Unadjusted FCF Model** | **BDT 109.04** | Overvalued |
| **Dividend Discount Model (DDM)** | **BDT 102.93** | Overvalued |
| **P/E Multiple (Industry Mean)** | **BDT 1,058.64** | Undervalued (Distorted by Outliers) |

> **Analyst Note:** Cash flow and dividend models indicate intrinsic fair values between BDT 102.93 and BDT 150.14.
> While the relative P/E model yields BDT 1,058.64, it is heavily skewed by industry outliers (e.g., IBP at 611x P/E).
> Adjusting for sector medians (25.43x) places fair value closer to current market levels.

## Financial Highlights & Ratio Analysis

* **ROE Progression (DuPont Breakdown):** Return on Equity expanded from **21.13% (2021)** to **28.09% (2025)**,
  driven primarily by Net Profit Margin expansion (from **5.62%** to **9.77%**) rather than leverage.
* **Capital Structure Shift:** Financial leverage decreased from **1.88x (2023)** to **1.42x (2025)**,
  reflecting a de-risked balance sheet financed predominantly by equity.
* **Liquidity:** Exceptional short-term buffer with a **Current Ratio of 4.77x** and a **Cash Ratio of 1.17x** in 2025.
